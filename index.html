How it got its name

Twenty-seven years on Warren Street

  1. 1999

    Bastille Day

    Opens at 321 Warren Street as Raymakers, a Belgian frites, burgers and mussels bar, when only a few businesses were on the street.

  2. 1999

    “Look for the red dot”

    Six months in, a regular suggests a new name, after the big red dot on the sign. It sticks.

  3. 2002

    Next door

    Red Dot takes over the building next door. A dance floor, a screening room and a garden with an outdoor fire follow.

  4. Weekends

    Brunch

    Weekend brunch joins dinner. Eggs Benedict on a potato latke is still on the menu.

  5. October

    Halloween

    Red Dot has hosted Halloween parties over the years.

  6. 2026

    Still here

    Dinner Friday to Tuesday, brunch Saturday and Sunday. Same address.
